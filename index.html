GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Ask Kieran a question ↗
What are the payment options?

Business Website is £20/month or £149 outright. Business Website Pro is £35/month or £249 outright. Each monthly package has an overview page before its Stripe checkout.

Can I see the website before it launches?

Yes. The process includes a design and review stage before launch. Message Kieran to discuss a free website demo and any changes you would like.

Are domain and hosting included?

The monthly website packages include your domain and hosting, with the setup and managed launch handled for you.

What do I need to send?

Your business name, services, contact details, confirmed service area, logo and work photographs. Send the material through WhatsApp and we will take it from there.

What is different about Pro?

Pro adds advanced layouts, enhanced service and location targeting, monthly SEO updates and maintenance, free website updates, matching social media branding and 30 days of posts.

Will a website guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search visibility depends on competition, content, technical foundations and ongoing work. We build the foundations; no specific ranking or number of enquiries is promised.

How do I request changes?

Send the proposed changes to Kieran. Confirm what is included in your chosen package, and discuss larger additions before work begins.
