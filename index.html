COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start.

What are the website payment options?

The Business Website package is £149 outright or £20/month. Business Website Pro is £249 outright or £35/month. Review the package details and checkout terms before paying.

What should I send to get started?

Your business name, services, contact details, confirmed service area, logo and work photographs. We use your real business information to plan the website.

Can I see the website before it launches?

The process includes a design and review stage before launch. You can discuss the details and requested adjustments with Kieran.

Do you build websites for trades?

Yes. Our supplied portfolio includes plumbing, garden services, cleaning and landscaping websites. We also work with other small and local service businesses across the UK.

What does the website include?

The package list includes mobile optimisation, Call and WhatsApp integration, enquiry forms, local SEO foundations and domain and hosting setup. Pro includes the additional services listed in its package.

Will you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search visibility depends on competition, useful content, technical foundations and ongoing work. A website does not guarantee a particular ranking or number of enquiries.

How do I request updates?

Send the proposed changes to Kieran. Confirm what is included in your chosen package and discuss larger additions before work begins.
